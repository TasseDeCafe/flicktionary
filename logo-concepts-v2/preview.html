Flicktionary — logo concepts v2

Five fresh directions, deliberately moving off the movie/ticket motif toward “study any content” (LingQ-style). Each concept has a detailed icon, a simplified small-size variant (shown at 64/32/16 px and in fake browser tabs), and a wordmark lockup.

A · Highlight warm amber · minimal-playful

A block of text with one word highlighted — the app's core gesture: you're reading anything, you tap a word, it lights up. The highlighter yellow becomes the brand accent.

The asterisk — the universal “there's a definition for this” symbol. Every word in your content carries an invisible footnote; Flicktionary reveals it. Bold, ownable, and it scales down better than anything else here.
