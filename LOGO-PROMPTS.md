# Flicktionary — Logo Generation Prompts

**Flicktionary** = "flick" (film) + "dictionary": a language-learning app where you
pull words/phrases ("chunks") out of movie subtitles and target-language text and turn
them into deep-dive study cards. So the logo wants to fuse **film/video** with
**words/language**, and stay legible as a tiny app icon (16–32px).

Each prompt below is a distinct concept, ready to paste into any modern image
generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram, etc.).

---

## Concept 1 — Speech bubble + play button
*(the strongest "video + language" mark)*

> Minimalist app icon logo: a rounded speech bubble with a small triangular play button cut out of its center. Single bold geometric shape, flat vector, two-color, high contrast, generous negative space, no text, no gradients. Centered on a solid background with rounded-square icon framing. Designed to stay legible at 16px favicon size.

## Concept 2 — Film frame holding a letter

> Minimalist app icon: a single film strip frame (square with two rows of sprocket holes on the sides) framing a bold capital letter "F" in the center. Flat vector, geometric, two flat colors, thick strokes, no gradients, no extra text. Clean negative space, rounded-square icon, optimized to read clearly when scaled down to a small icon.

## Concept 3 — Highlighter + subtitle line
*(matches the app's core action: highlighting chunks)*

> Minimalist app icon: a short underline/subtitle bar with one segment highlighted in a bright accent color, sitting inside a rounded movie-clapperboard silhouette. Flat vector, bold simple shapes, two or three flat colors, no gradients, no text. Strong contrast, lots of negative space, must remain recognizable at tiny icon sizes.

## Concept 4 — Play button made of a folded dictionary page

> Minimalist app icon: a triangular play button formed by a single folded page/bookmark, suggesting both "film play" and "a page of words". Pure geometric vector, flat two-tone, thick clean edges, no gradients, no lettering. Centered with even margins in a rounded square, designed for crisp rendering at small sizes.

## Concept 5 — Monogram "F" as a play triangle

> Minimalist monogram app icon: the letter "F" constructed so its counter-space forms a sideways play triangle. Single bold glyph, flat solid color on contrasting background, geometric, no gradients, no outline noise. Heavy stroke weight for legibility, rounded-square icon, must stay clear and balanced at 16–32px.

---

## Tips for whichever you generate

- Add `--no text, gradient, photorealism, drop shadow, 3D` (or your tool's negative-prompt equivalent) to keep it flat and icon-safe.
- Ask for it **on a transparent or solid flat background** so you can drop it into the rounded-square app frame yourself.
- Generate at large size, then **preview at 32px** before committing — if the play triangle or letter blurs together, simplify further (fewer colors, thicker strokes).
- A two-color palette reads best as a favicon. Pick one strong brand accent + one neutral.
